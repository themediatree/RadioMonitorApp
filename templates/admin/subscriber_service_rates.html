How multipliers work

Cost = base_tokens × multiplier, where base_tokens = stations × hours (minimum 24 h).
A multiplier of 0.50 means this subscriber pays half the normal token rate for that service. A multiplier of 1.00 means they pay the standard rate. Values above 1.00 are valid (premium pricing).

Registration fee is a flat token amount charged once per commercial spot registered, in addition to the per-hour monitoring cost. Leave blank for no flat fee (default). Applies to both the app dashboard and API registrations.
