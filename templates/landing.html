Five core capabilities

Built for broadcast. Designed for intelligence.

Every feature runs continuously across your monitored stations, with results available in real time through the NOCTIV dashboard.

Remote Broadcast Monitoring
Continuous, unattended monitoring of live radio streams — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. NOCTIV captures every broadcast minute and makes it searchable, auditable, and provable. Whether you're tracking a single station or a national network, every second is accounted for.
→ Always on
Transcription & Content Archive
Every broadcast is automatically transcribed using AI speech recognition. Searchable archives let you find any spoken word, phrase, or topic across months of content in seconds. Play back any segment with live word-level highlighting — like karaoke for radio. Export for compliance reporting, research, or legal evidence.
→ Karaoke-style word playback
Commercial Monitoring & Verification
Register your commercials once. NOCTIV's acoustic fingerprint engine detects every airing across all monitored stations — capturing the exact date, time, and duration of each broadcast. For presenter-read scripts, the pipeline transcribes the broadcast and matches it word-for-word against your registered text — flagging confirmed airings with a karaoke-style transcript so you know exactly what was said. Every detection produces a tamper-evident evidence package delivered to your dashboard within 60 seconds of broadcast.
→ Evidence-grade proof of broadcast
Music Monitoring & Identification
Every song played on every monitored station is identified in real time using audio recognition. Track playlist compliance, verify royalty obligations, analyse music trends, and generate accurate play-count reports — all without manual logging or station cooperation.
→ Every track. Every station.
Word & Phrase Detection
Subscribe to any keyword, brand name, phrase, or topic and receive an alert the moment it's spoken on radio — with a timestamped audio clip as evidence. Monitor brand mentions, competitor activity, political messaging, regulatory announcements, crisis keywords, or any content that matters to your organisation. Results arrive within minutes of broadcast, not hours.
→ Real-time alerts

Who it's built for

Six industries. One infrastructure.

NOCTIV serves every organisation that depends on what's said, played, or advertised on radio.

Advertising & Media Verification
Prove that paid radio commercials aired at contracted times. Eliminate billing disputes and provide advertisers with timestamped, clip-verified proof of broadcast.
Brand & Reputation Intelligence
Track every mention of your brand, product, executives, and competitors across radio. Know what's being said the moment it's broadcast.
Broadcast Compliance & Regulation
Monitor adherence to broadcasting regulations, mandatory announcements, and content requirements. Searchable archives provide audit-ready evidence for regulatory bodies.
Music & Content Analytics
Verify playlist compliance, track song airplay for royalty reporting, and analyse broadcast content trends across your station network.
Government, Security & Situational Awareness
Monitor broadcasts for crisis keywords, emergency information, political messaging, and regulatory compliance in real time. NOCTIV's Frequency Spectrum Analysis (FSA) module extends coverage to physical RF monitoring — detecting unlicensed transmitters and spectrum violations for ICASA enforcement and field inspection teams.

How it works

From broadcast to intelligence in minutes.

STEP 1
Connect your stations
NOCTIV connects to your radio stations' live streams. No hardware installation required. Coverage begins immediately — 24/7, without interruption.
STEP 2
Register what matters
Upload your commercials, subscribe to songs, or set keyword alerts. NOCTIV starts watching for your content within 60 seconds of registration.
STEP 3
Receive verified intelligence
Every detection is timestamped, tamper-evident, and delivered to your dashboard within 60 seconds of broadcast. Play back any clip with word-level karaoke highlighting, download a Proof of Broadcast certificate, or pull data via API — on demand.
